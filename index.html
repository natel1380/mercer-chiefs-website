Tier I AAA — THF

Tier 1 National Bound & Youth

Full-season Tier 1 AAA program competing in the Tier 1 Hockey Federation (THF). Includes 3+ ice sessions per week, professional skating coach Oktay Armgan, video analysis, and off-ice conditioning.

  • Birth Years: 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016
  • Arena: Ice Land Skating Center — Hamilton, NJ
  • Showcase Schedule: 5 Major THF & SuperSeries Showcases
  • Equipment Package: Custom CCM Chiefs gloves, pants, bag, jerseys
Tier II — AHF

Tier II Competitive & Development

Competitive travel hockey competing in the Atlantic Hockey Federation (AHF). Focused on continuous player skill acquisition, positional awareness, team culture, and positive character building.

  • Birth Years: 2008 through 2018 (8U Mites through 18U)
  • Divisions: Lemieux, McDavid, Gretzky & AA Conferences
  • Goaltender Training: Terry Jarkowsky Goalie Academy included
  • Tournaments: Regional District Playoffs & AHF Championships
